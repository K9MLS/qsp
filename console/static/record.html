Transmissions

—

This exists for net control. A station taking check-ins who misses a callsign can read it back here afterwards, which the live list could not do — it held fifty entries and lost them on every restart. Text messages arrive as one-frame data bursts and are marked as such, so they do not bury the voice traffic. How long the record is kept is dmr.calls.retain in the configuration.
