Overview

What this instance does

QSP is a DMR master. Peers on a talkgroup hear each other, and bridges additionally move traffic between talkgroups, on a schedule or on PTT. Forwarding and scheduling are configured per instance and may be switched off; the panels below show what this instance is actually doing.
