Sent and received are counted separately because one is not evidence of the other. A link that has sent thousands of frames and received none is either working perfectly on a quiet network or unauthenticated at the far end, and no single number tells those apart. Rejected is the one to watch: a steady trickle almost always means the two ends disagree about the passphrase.

Offer a peering

A peering carries traffic between this network and another club's. Neither end can carry anything until both administrators hold the same passphrase, so this generates an offer, not a connection — nothing happens until the other operator accepts it and sends you their own details back. The passphrase is shown once here and is not stored anywhere you can read it again.
