Links

Links to other networks. A link is an agreement between two administrators: neither end can carry traffic without a passphrase the other gave them.

Sign in as an administrator. A link names somebody else's server and whether their passphrase is working, which is theirs to disclose rather than ours.

Configured links

Sent and received are counted separately because one is not evidence of the other. A link that has sent thousands of frames and received none is either working perfectly on a quiet network or unauthenticated at the far end, and no single number tells those apart. Rejected is the one to watch: a steady trickle almost always means the two ends disagree about the passphrase.
