Identity

The identifier is this server's name on the network, generated once and never changed. It is shown because two servers may choose the same display name, and then it is the only thing that tells them apart.

    Sessions

    Send a text

    A group text, composed by this server and sent to everybody on the talkgroup: every hotspot on it, every Motorola repeater, and wherever the bridges carry it. Nothing acknowledges a group text, so the only proof it arrived is a radio's display.

    Not the ID of the radio you are watching: a radio commonly ignores a message that claims to come from itself.

    For testing: send to this one hotspot and nowhere else. Leave it empty to send to the whole talkgroup.

    Backup and restore

    A backup carries this server's settings and its identifier, and no passwords — so it is safe to email, to keep with your other files, or to send to somebody helping you. It lists the credentials it cannot carry, so a restore has a checklist rather than a mystery.
