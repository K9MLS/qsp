    Bridges

    Peers on the same talkgroup already hear each other with no bridge at all — that is what forwarding does. A bridge is for joining talkgroups that differ, so traffic on one is carried to the other. An endpoint is a peer, a talkgroup and a timeslot; leave the peer blank to mean every peer carrying that talkgroup.

    —

    Each bridge joins two or more endpoints. An endpoint is a peer, a talkgroup and a timeslot — leave the peer blank to mean every peer carrying that talkgroup.

    Schedule

    A window turns a bridge on for part of a week. A bridge named by any window is controlled entirely by the schedule and is off outside its windows whatever its own setting says — which is worth knowing before a net fails to open rather than afterwards. The timezone must be an IANA name such as America/Chicago: an abbreviation cannot express "20:00 local all year" across a daylight-saving change.

    A window turns a bridge on for part of a week — a net on a Tuesday evening, say. A bridge named by any window is controlled entirely by the schedule, so it is off outside its windows whatever its own setting says.
